About Me

My name is Shahan. I'm a student who aspires to be a Game Designer for Japanese company Bandai Namco. I've taught myself game design for 1 year now and with every idea that comes to mind, if it's good enough, I like to keep it for future work if execution is within reach. Some of my work has involved the idea for an improved version of Pac-Man's 3D adventure game "Pac-Man World 2 Re-Pac" where my idea for improvisation may prove to be useful for a potential remake of it's sequel, with my best work up to date is an original game idea for an online Trading Card Game(TCG). Despite my interest in Game Design, I also like to teach myself programming to be able to instantiate my vision to life, and the game engine I currently use is the Unity Engine.
